About

We approach investing as the careful allocation of capital, grounded in fundamental analysis, patience and discipline.

H&N Gestion is an independent investment company engaged principally in the ownership, management and trading of securities using its own capital.

Our work is centered on understanding businesses, assessing intrinsic value and taking investment decisions with a long-term perspective while maintaining rigorous risk control.

Investment Philosophy

Fundamental research first. Valuation second. Discipline always.

01

Fundamental Research

We seek to understand the economics, competitive position, management and long-term prospects of each business before forming an investment view.

02

Valuation Discipline

Investment decisions are made with reference to estimated intrinsic value, expected return and the margin of safety available at the prevailing market price.

03

Independent Judgment

We place emphasis on primary information, independent analysis and a willingness to differ from consensus when supported by evidence.

04

Risk Management

Capital preservation is integral to portfolio construction. Position sizing, liquidity and downside risk are considered alongside expected return.

Business

Proprietary investment. Our capital, our decisions, our risk.

The company’s principal business is the holding, management and trading of securities for its own account, primarily domestic listed equities.

We aim to pursue rational and disciplined investment decisions, applying accumulated knowledge in corporate analysis, valuation and portfolio management.

Compliance

No external capital. No client assets. Proprietary investment only.

H&N Gestion does not solicit investment from the public or from external investors, does not accept subscriptions or contributions of capital for investment purposes, and does not manage assets on behalf of clients or third parties.

All securities transactions are undertaken solely for our own account using our own capital. We conduct our activities in strict compliance with the Financial Instruments and Exchange Act of Japan and all other applicable laws and regulations.

Yamanashi

Building an investment culture outside Tokyo.

In Japan, investment-related companies, financial institutions and investment professionals are overwhelmingly concentrated in Tokyo. We have deliberately chosen to establish our base in Yamanashi.

We believe investment expertise and opportunity need not be confined to the capital. From Yamanashi, H&N Gestion aims to become an early example of an independent investment partnership rooted in a regional community, demonstrating that disciplined investment activity can be built and sustained outside Japan's traditional financial centre.

Over time, we hope our presence can contribute, even in a modest way, to the accumulation of investment knowledge, talent and business opportunities in the region.
